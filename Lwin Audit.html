3. Financial statements and source records

Attach the ledger, enter the trial balance, then complete the financial statements in report format.

Linked financial statementsWhen the Statement of Financial Position or Profit or Loss values change, the system rebuilds the Statement of Changes in Equity and a provisional IAS 7 indirect-method cash-flow statement. The cash-flow derivation is a reconciliation aid only: acquisitions, disposals, non-cash lease movements, dividends and financing transactions must still be verified against the general ledger and supporting schedules.

5. Preliminary audit conclusion

Assess evidence, misstatements, scope limitations and going concern before drafting the report.

This module produces a preliminary drafting recommendation only. It evaluates the answers entered; it does not perform audit procedures or verify uploaded files. An audit opinion may be signed only by the responsible independent auditor after completing the audit and documenting professional judgements.
Complete the conclusion fields to calculate a preliminary draft-opinion assessment.
Final review completedI understand that the calculated assessment is not an issued opinion and must be reviewed and approved by the engagement partner.
No management or shareholder recommendations are included because the generated document is structured as an independent auditor’s report and financial statements.
