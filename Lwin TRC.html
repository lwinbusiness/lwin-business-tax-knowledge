1. TRC ဆိုတာဘာလဲ? / What is a Tax Residency Certificate?
မြန်မာ

TRC ဆိုတာ FTA က သတ်မှတ်ထားသော period အတွက် applicant ကို UAE tax resident အဖြစ် အတည်ပြုပေးသော certificate ဖြစ်သည်။ Certificate တစ်စောင်ရခြင်းသည် တခြားနိုင်ငံတွင် tax exemption အလိုအလျောက်ရမည်ဟု မဆိုလိုပါ။ Receiving country, DTA article, income type နှင့် beneficial ownership စည်းကမ်းများကို သီးခြားစစ်ရသည်။

ENGLISH

A TRC is issued by the FTA to confirm UAE tax-resident status for a selected period. It does not automatically guarantee foreign tax relief; the receiving country, treaty article, income and other conditions must still be tested.

Certificate useMeaningMain warning
For DTA purposesUsed to claim benefits under an effective UAE double-tax treatySelect the correct treaty country and review its residence definition
For purposes other than DTADomestic/general proof of UAE tax residenceMay not be accepted as a treaty certificate by a foreign authority
International form attestationFTA stamps another jurisdiction’s form linked to the TRCCountry, period, completion and signature must match
Certificate period: Current tax period, prior tax period or another selected 12-month period. Future period and a period longer than 12 months are not allowed.
2. Choose the correct application route / မှန်ကန်သော route ရွေးခြင်း

Domestic / Non-DTA

General proof; not based on a treaty claim.

Treaty / DTA

For an effective treaty with a selected country.

Commercial Activities

A different certificate/service; do not confuse with personal TRC.

Logical error to avoid: Domestic TRC ရရှိထားတာကို Treaty TRC အစားမသုံးပါနှင့်။ Treaty application မှာ relevant DTA ကိုရွေးပြီး treaty-specific evidence တင်ရနိုင်ပါတယ်။
3. Natural-person tax-residency tests / Natural Person အရည်အချင်း
Route under UAE domestic rulesCore testTypical evidence
Case 1 — 183 days or morePhysically present in UAE for at least 183 days in a consecutive 12-month periodEID/visa or passport; entry-exit report; explanation of UAE presence
Case 2 — 90 to 182 daysAt least 90 days plus UAE/GCC nationality or valid UAE residence permit, and employment/business or permanent home conditionIdentity/travel plus salary/labour/business evidence OR permanent-home evidence
Case 3 — Primary home and centre of interestsUsual/primary residence and financial/personal interests are in UAEDetailed statement; home, family, banking, social/professional and income evidence

Case 2 supporting evidence

Employment: UAE salary certificate, labour contract or continuing income relationship
Business: trade licence, ownership and evidence of carrying on business in UAE
Permanent home: certified tenancy/long-term lease, landlord statement, or title deed plus utility bill

Case 3 centre-of-interests evidence

Written explanation of why UAE is the centre of financial and personal interests
Family EIDs and entry-exit reports
Bank statements, club/professional memberships and social ties
Primary-home evidence and comparison with homes/stays in other countries
Salary, shares, trade licence, savings or other source-of-income evidence
Important: Natural person may apply as soon as the relevant tax-residency criterion is met. The 12-month certificate period does not always have to be finished first.
4. TRC for purposes other than DTA / Domestic Purpose
မြန်မာ

Domestic/non-DTA application သည် Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022 အရ UAE tax residence အတည်ပြုရန်ဖြစ်သည်။ Document list သည် 183-day, 90-day သို့မဟုတ် centre-of-interests case ဘယ်ဟာကိုသုံးသည်ပေါ် မူတည်သည်။

ENGLISH

The non-DTA application proves UAE tax residence under Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022. Documents depend on whether the applicant relies on the 183-day, 90-day or centre-of-interests route.

Minimum starting file — 183-day route

Valid Emirates ID and residence visa, or passport where applicable
Official entry and exit report for the selected consecutive 12-month period
Signed declaration explaining physical presence for 183 days or more
Selected certificate period and reason for request
UAE Pass / EmaraTax access and active registered email
User’s April 2026 example: EID + passport + entry/exit statement were submitted; FTA processing was about 5 working days. Treat this as a case example, not a universal document limit.
5. TRC for DTA / Treaty purposes
မြန်မာ

Treaty TRC မတင်မီ target country နဲ့ UAE ကြား DTA အကျိုးသက်ရောက်နေမှု၊ treaty residence definition၊ tie-breaker rules နှင့် income article ကိုစစ်ရသည်။ FTA သို့ basic residence evidence အပြင် treaty ကတောင်းသည့် supporting evidence ကိုတင်ရနိုင်သည်။

ENGLISH

Before a treaty TRC application, verify that the DTA is in force and review its residence definition, tie-breaker and relevant income article. Treaty-specific evidence may be required in addition to standard residence documents.

Typical natural-person document pack

Emirates ID and residence visa, or passport plus official entry-exit report
Proof of UAE residence: title deed or tenancy and utility evidence where applicable
Source of UAE income: salary certificate, labour contract, business or investment evidence
Six-month bank statement where relevant to the facts/request
Target treaty country and reason for treaty relief
Foreign authority’s international form, completed and signed, if attestation is required
Not automatic: Title deed + bank statement + salary certificate does not by itself prove treaty residence. The concerned DTA may apply a different residence definition or tie-breaker.

International form matching rules

ItemMust match
CountrySame country selected in the DTA TRC application
PeriodSame 12-month period as the TRC
CompletionForm fully completed by applicant; FTA does not complete it
SignatureApplicant signs; juridical applicant also stamps where required
6. Entry/Exit Statement / Travel Report from Amer
မြန်မာ

Entry/Exit Statement သည် selected consecutive 12-month period အတွင်း UAE ဝင်/ထွက်နေ့များကို သက်သေပြရန်အသုံးပြုသည်။ Report period က TRC period ကိုပြည့်စုံစွာ cover လုပ်ရပြီး passport data နှင့်ကိုက်ညီရသည်။

ENGLISH

The entry/exit statement supports physical-presence days. Its period should fully cover the selected TRC period and match the passport identity.

Offline Amer process

4Pay center fee
5Receive and verify PDF

Missing movementEscalate before TRC submission
Cost note: User’s April 2026 Amer example was AED 238.90 plus bank charge AED 2.38, with 24–48 hours. This is an external historical example; current Amer/immigration fees must be reconfirmed.
7. Online portal steps / FTA Platform လုပ်ငန်းစဉ်
Step 1 — Access
Open EmaraTax/TRC platform and sign in through the available UAE Pass/account route.
Step 2 — Service
Choose Other Services → Tax Residency Certificate.
Step 3 — TRN
Select applicant’s Corporate Tax TRN if applicable; otherwise choose No TRN. CT TRN can reduce the certificate review fee.
Step 4 — Type
Select DTA purpose or purposes other than DTA. For DTA, select the relevant country.
Step 5 — Period
Select current/prior tax period or another period of no more than 12 months; no future period.
Step 6 — Applicant data
Complete Emirates ID, passport, visa, address and contact details exactly as shown in evidence.
Step 7 — Evidence
Upload travel report and route-specific residence, employment, income, home and treaty evidence.
Step 8 — Options
Request hard copy and/or international-form attestation if needed. Verify delivery address in UAE.
Step 9 — Pay and submit
Settle submission and review/issuance fee in full, then submit. Fees are non-refundable if rejected.
Step 10 — Download
After approval, download icon appears and certificate is sent to registered email. Hard copy is couriered if requested.
Payment failure control: User’s prior experience indicates a failed payment attempt may require waiting before retrying. Do not create duplicate requests immediately; first check payment/transaction status and portal instructions.

Open generic FTA TRC platform — Do not save or reuse a request-specific URL containing another application UUID.
