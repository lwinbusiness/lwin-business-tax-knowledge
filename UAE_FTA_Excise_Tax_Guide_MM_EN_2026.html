ENGLISH

Excise Tax is not tested only at sale. Liability can arise on import, production, release, stockpiling, loss/destruction and product-registration events.

Official-source conflict: FTA Cabinet-linked announcement states AED 0.79/litre for medium-sugar drinks, while another FTA topic page displays AED 0.97. This guide uses AED 0.79 as the calculator default but requires verification against Cabinet Decision No. 197 of 2025 and the active EmaraTax product record before filing.
1. Excise Tax ဆိုတာဘာလဲ? / What is Excise Tax?
မြန်မာ

Excise Tax သည် ကျန်းမာရေး/ပတ်ဝန်းကျင်ကို ထိခိုက်စေနိုင်သော သတ်မှတ်ကုန်ပစ္စည်းများအပေါ်ကောက်ခံသော indirect tax ဖြစ်သည်။ VAT လို supply အားလုံးမဟုတ်ဘဲ product category နှင့် taxable event ကိုအခြေခံသည်။

ENGLISH

Excise Tax is an indirect tax on specified goods considered harmful to health or the environment. Unlike VAT, it is driven by product classification and taxable events.

Excise liability = Applicable rate/amount × excise price or taxable volume/quantity
Do not confuse: Excise Tax paid does not remove VAT. VAT can apply to the selling price including relevant taxes under the VAT valuation rules.

6. Excise Price & 100% Calculation / Excise Price တွက်နည်း
မြန်မာ

100% products အတွက် excise price သည် FTA published price နှင့် importer/producer သတ်မှတ် retail price (tax-exclusive basis) တို့အနက် higher amount ဖြစ်နိုင်သည်။ Retail price VAT/Excise-inclusive ဖြစ်လျှင် embedded tax ကိုမှန်ကန်စွာခွဲရသည်။

ENGLISH

For 100% products, the excise price is generally the higher of the FTA-published price and designated retail selling price on a tax-exclusive basis.

For a 100% excise product where retail price includes Excise Tax only:
Excise Tax = 50% of the stated retail price
Example: Excise-inclusive retail basis AED 20 → pre-excise value AED 10; Excise Tax AED 10, subject to official price/value rules and VAT interaction.
RiskResolution
FTA price higher than label basisUse higher statutory excise price
Promotion/discount changes retail realityReview designated retail price and product record
Pack size changesCreate/update SKU and price evidence before movement
VAT and Excise mixedMaintain a tax bridge showing pre-tax, Excise and VAT
7. Sweetened Drinks — 2026 Volumetric Model
မြန်မာ

1 January 2026 မှစပြီး sweetened drinks ကို retail price ရာခိုင်နှုန်းဖြင့်မတွက်တော့ဘဲ total sugar/other sweeteners per 100ml နှင့် final drink volume အပေါ် AED per litre ဖြင့်တွက်သည်။ Powder/concentrate ကို label preparation instructions အရ ပြုလုပ်နိုင်သည့် total servings/litres ဖြင့်တွက်ရသည်။

ENGLISH

From 1 January 2026, sweetened drinks use AED per litre based on total sugar/other sweeteners per 100ml and final drink volume. Concentrates and powders use the drink yield under label preparation instructions.

CategorySugar contentAmount/litreEvidence
High≥8g/100mlAED 1.09Certificate; otherwise high may be default
Medium≥5g and <8g/100mlAED 0.79 used here; verify official conflictCabinet decision and active product record
Low<5g/100mlAED 0Certificate still critical
Artificial-only/qualifying low combinationWithin current category conditionsAED 0Composition and certificate

Common exclusions/other treatment

  • Energy drinks remain 100% and are not under volumetric model.
  • 100% natural fruit/vegetable juice with no added sugar/other sweetener can be outside sweetened-drink definition.
  • Milk/dairy, baby formula/food and qualifying special dietary/medical drinks have specified exclusions.
  • Open, non-hermetically sealed drinks prepared in restaurants for direct consumption may be excluded subject to conditions.
Carbonated drinks: Carbonated drink is no longer a separate category merely because carbonated. Test sweetened-drink composition or energy-drink status.
8. Import Process / တင်သွင်းမှုလုပ်ငန်းစဉ်

Tax-paid import

Suspended movement

  1. Entity and product registration complete.
  2. Customs code/TRN/product identity linked.
  3. Determine tax-paid clearance or permitted suspended movement.
  4. Declare quantity, price/volume and calculate Excise.
  5. Account for import VAT separately.
  6. Reconcile customs, warehouse, inventory and return.
Blocker: Unregistered/mismatched product, missing conformity certificate or incorrect DTS can stop customs clearance.

10. Warehouse Keeper & Designated Zone
မြန်မာ

Excise Designated Zone သည် ordinary Free Zone မဟုတ်ပါ။ FTA registration, warehouse keeper, physical/security controls, financial guarantee, inventory system နှင့် renewal requirements ရှိသည်။

ENGLISH

An Excise Designated Zone is not an ordinary free zone. It requires FTA registration, a warehouse keeper, controls, financial guarantee, inventory systems and renewal.

Zone and keeper registrations current
Financial guarantee adequate
Separate owner/SKU/tax-status stock ledger
Every receipt, transfer, release, export and loss authorised
Physical counts and variances investigated
Renewal and amendment calendar
Failure condition: Missing, consumed or improperly removed goods can be treated as released, creating Excise liability.

Operating Control System / လက်တွေ့ထိန်းချုပ်မှု

Daily

  • Post imports, production, transfers, release, export and loss by SKU/quantity.
  • Block unregistered products and expired/missing certificates.
  • Reconcile DTS for affected tobacco packs.

Monthly

  • Opening + receipts/production − closing = every movement.
  • Customs-to-product-to-warehouse reconciliation.
  • Excise price and sweetened-drink certificate exception report.
  • Return review, payment and archived evidence.
