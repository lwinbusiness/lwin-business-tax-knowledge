UAE MOHRE System & Employment Visa Guide

The Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) regulates private-sector labour matters, including establishment records, job offers, work permits, employment contracts, the Wage Protection System (WPS) and labour relations. Immigration and residence procedures are completed through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP), or through the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs – Dubai (GDRFA Dubai) for Dubai cases; MOHRE does not complete the residence process by itself.

MOHRE သည် private-sector ကုမ္ပဏီများ၏ establishment record၊ job offer၊ work permit၊ employment contract၊ WPS salary နှင့် labour relations များကို စီမံသည်။ Residence visa အပိုင်းကို MOHRE တစ်ခုတည်းမလုပ်ဘဲ ICP သို့မဟုတ် Dubai တွင် GDRFA နှင့်ဆက်လက်လုပ်ရသည်။

First Decision: Mainland or Free Zone?

Mainland Private-Sector Company

MOHRE normally handles job offer, quota, work permit and labour contract. ICP/GDRFA handles entry permit, status/residence and Emirates ID integration.

Mainland company တွင် job offer၊ quota၊ work permit နှင့် labour contract ကို MOHRE မှလုပ်ပြီး entry permit/residence ကို ICP သို့မဟုတ် GDRFA မှလုပ်သည်။

Free Zone Company

The relevant Free Zone authority normally manages its employment permit and establishment channel. Immigration still connects to ICP/GDRFA. Do not automatically use the mainland MOHRE route.

Free Zone company တွင် သက်ဆိုင်ရာ Free Zone authority ၏ employment permit system ကိုအသုံးပြုရတတ်သည်။ Mainland MOHRE process ကိုအလိုအလျောက်မသုံးရ။

Critical: An employment visa is not one single application. It combines labour approval/work permit, immigration residence procedures, medical fitness and Emirates ID. Working on a visit or tourist visa is prohibited.

MOHRE’s published service target is two working days when the application is complete. The system issues a shortfall notification if correction is required.

Pay work-permit and worker-protection charges

MOHRE payment screen Pay AED 50 application fee plus the two-year issuance fee based on establishment category: AED 250, AED 1,200 or AED 3,450. Add the applicable bank guarantee or worker-protection insurance.

Work Bundle / ICPDubai: GDRFA/Amer Continue to the linked immigration application. Check passport, work-permit reference, establishment card and sponsor details before payment.

If the employee is already in the UAE, do not use an overseas-arrival assumption; the case may need an inside-country status adjustment and additional fee.

Employee enters UAE or completes status adjustment

Verify the entry/status document and deadline. The employee must not start work merely because a tourist/visit status exists.

Medical fitness

EHS / Dubai authorised medical centre / emirate authority Employee aged 18+ attends physically with the required application and identity documents. EHS currently lists Category A at AED 260 plus service-centre fees and a 24-hour result target.

Emirates ID and biometrics

ICP / integrated Work Bundle Submit the ID application and complete fingerprint/biometrics if requested. ICP lists resident ID issuance at AED 100 per residence year plus AED 100 smart-service fee.

Health insurance and residence issuance

Insurer + ICP/GDRFA Arrange compliant insurance for the applicable emirate/category, then submit final residence issuance. Dubai GDRFA currently lists AED 200 residence fee, AED 10 Knowledge Dirham, AED 10 Innovation Dirham, AED 20 delivery and AED 500 extra for inside-country processing.

Final verification and payroll activation

Download/verify the digital residence, Emirates ID status, work permit and MOHRE contract. Confirm UID, name, passport, occupation and sponsor consistency. Add the employee to payroll/WPS and preserve every receipt and approval.

Documents and Requirements

Employee documents

  • Clear passport copy, normally valid for at least six months
  • Recent colour photograph with white background
  • Signed official MOHRE job offer
  • Attested/recognised academic certificate where the skill level requires it
  • Professional licence for regulated professions
  • Home-country national ID copy for nationalities specifically required by MOHRE
  • Existing visa, UID and cancellation documents for an in-country transfer

Company requirements

  • Valid trade licence and active establishment/labour file
  • No blocking violations or suspension
  • Available electronic quota where required
  • Occupation compatible with licensed business activity
  • Application submitted by the authorised signatory
  • Bank guarantee or approved worker-protection insurance, where applicable

After entry / in UAE

  • Medical fitness result
  • Emirates ID application and biometrics when requested
  • Compliant health insurance where required
  • Immigration residence issuance documents
  • Final employment contract/work-permit records
  • WPS salary setup after employment begins
Qualification rule: MOHRE’s service page states that skill levels 1–2 require bachelor’s degree or higher, levels 3–4 diploma or higher, level 5 high-school certificate, and levels 6–9 no certificate. Regulated jobs still need the relevant professional licence. Verification/recognition requirements may change by profession and system integration.

Contract and Labour-Law Essentials

Contract model

Private-sector contracts are fixed-term and renewable. Work models include full-time, part-time, temporary, flexible, remote and job-sharing arrangements where applicable.

Probation

Maximum six months. Employer termination during probation generally requires 14 days’ written notice. Changing to another UAE employer during probation generally requires at least one month’s written notice.

Normal hours

Generally eight hours per day or 48 hours per week, subject to sector and prescribed exceptions. A break is required after five consecutive hours and is not counted as working time.

Overtime

Generally limited to two extra hours daily. Ordinary overtime uses basic salary plus at least 25%; certain night overtime may be plus 50%, subject to exceptions.

Weekly/public rest

At least one paid weekly rest day. Public-holiday work requires the legally prescribed substitute rest or additional payment.

Non-compete

Must protect a legitimate business interest and be limited by time, place and type of work. The statutory maximum restrictive period is two years.

Leave and Employee Entitlements

Annual leave

Generally 30 days per year after one year of service; two days per month where service exceeds six months but is less than one year. Accrual and carry-forward/payment rules must be applied carefully.

Service တစ်နှစ်ပြည့်လျှင် annual leave 30 days ရရှိပြီး 6 months ကျော် 1 year မပြည့်သေးလျှင် တစ်လ 2 days နှုန်းဖြစ်သည်။

Sick leave

After probation, up to 90 days per year of service: first 15 days full pay, next 30 days half pay and remaining 45 days unpaid, subject to medical evidence and exclusions.

Probation ပြီးနောက် တစ်နှစ်လျှင် 90 days အထိ—15 days full pay၊ နောက် 30 days half pay၊ ကျန် 45 days unpaid ဖြစ်သည်။

Maternity and parental leave

Maternity leave is 60 days: 45 days full pay and 15 days half pay, with additional prescribed leave in qualifying medical or child-disability cases. Parental leave is five working days for either parent, subject to the statutory timing conditions.

Maternity leave 60 days တွင် 45 days full pay နှင့် 15 days half pay ဖြစ်သည်။ Parental leave ကို သတ်မှတ်ကာလအတွင်း မိဘတစ်ဦးဦးက 5 working days ရနိုင်သည်။

Bereavement, study and Hajj leave

Bereavement leave depends on the relationship. Eligible employees studying at an accredited UAE institution may receive 10 working days’ paid study leave after two years’ service. Unpaid Hajj leave may be granted once during service for up to 30 days.

Bereavement leave သည် relation အလိုက်ကွာသည်။ UAE accredited institution တွင်တက်နေပြီး service 2 years ပြည့်သူသည် examination အတွက် paid study leave 10 working days ရနိုင်သည်။ Hajj အတွက် service တစ်လျှောက်တစ်ကြိမ် unpaid leave 30 days အထိရနိုင်သည်။

Emiratisation and Establishment Compliance

  • Companies with 50 or more employees are generally subject to growth targets for Emiratis in skilled positions, assessed semi-annually.
  • Selected companies with 20–49 employees in specified sectors may also be subject to hiring/retention targets.
  • Fake Emiratisation creates serious legal, financial and classification exposure.
  • From 1 January 2026, MOHRE announced a minimum monthly salary of AED 6,000 for Emiratis in the private sector, with existing contracts required to be adjusted by 30 June 2026 and measures starting 1 July 2026 for non-compliance.
  • Emirati employment also connects with Nafis and pension/social-security registration and contribution requirements.
Do not use a generic target calculation: Headcount, skilled-job definition, selected-sector status, group fragmentation and prior Emirati retention can materially change the obligation. Check the company’s actual MOHRE dashboard.

Complaints, Inspections and Dispute Control

  • Employees and employers can use MOHRE channels for labour complaints and enquiries; the MOHRE call centre is 600590000 and the Labour Claims and Advisory Centre is 80084.
  • Keep signed offer/contract, salary records, attendance, leave approvals, warnings, performance evidence, settlement calculations and cancellation acknowledgments.
  • Do not fabricate WPS payments, employment, attendance or Emiratisation records. Digital systems can reconcile cross-government data.
  • Where a dispute affects cancellation or a new permit, confirm the actual MOHRE case status rather than relying on verbal statements.

Common Employment Scenarios

Case 1 — Accountant recruited from outside UAE

The employer first checks quota, occupation/activity compatibility and academic-document requirements. Both parties sign the official job offer; the employer applies for the overseas work permit, then immigration entry/residence, medical and Emirates ID procedures follow.

Company က quota၊ Accountant profession နှင့် activity ကိုက်ညီမှု၊ degree document requirement ကိုအရင်စစ်ရမည်။ Job offer လက်မှတ်ထိုးပြီး overseas work permit၊ entry/residence၊ medical နှင့် Emirates ID ကိုဆက်လုပ်ရမည်။

Case 2 — Employee transferring inside UAE

The old work permit/residence status must be checked and cancellation coordinated. The new employer uses the applicable transfer/new-permit route. MOHRE’s transfer service states the new application should be filed within 90 days after cancellation of the previous work permit.

အလုပ်ဟောင်း၏ work permit/residence status နှင့် cancellation ကိုစစ်ပြီး employer အသစ်က transfer/new-permit route ကိုသုံးရမည်။ Previous work permit cancellation ပြီး 90 days အတွင်း လျှောက်ရမည်ဟု MOHRE transfer service ကဖော်ပြထားသည်။

Case 3 — Golden Visa holder joining a company

The employee keeps the self-sponsored Golden Visa but the employer normally obtains the relevant MOHRE work permit. It is not a standard employer-sponsored residence-visa process.

Employee က Golden Visa ကိုဆက်ထိန်းထားပြီး company က သက်ဆိုင်ရာ MOHRE work permit ကိုလျှောက်ရသည်။ Employer-sponsored residence visa အသစ်မဟုတ်ပါ။

Case 4 — Free Zone employee

The company should use its Free Zone portal and authority rules for employment approval rather than assuming the MOHRE mainland workflow. Medical, Emirates ID and immigration procedures still apply through the relevant channels.

Free Zone company သည် MOHRE mainland workflow ကိုမယူဆဘဲ Free Zone portal/authority rules ကိုအသုံးပြုရမည်။ Medical၊ Emirates ID နှင့် immigration process များတော့ ဆက်လက်လိုအပ်သည်။

What Should Be Stopped, Started and Controlled

Stop: Letting an employee work on a visit visa; selecting a profession only to reduce cost; assuming a degree is never needed; treating the residence visa and MOHRE work permit as the same approval.
Start: Check establishment status and quota first, verify occupation and qualification before issuing the offer, and keep one reconciled file for MOHRE, immigration, medical, Emirates ID and WPS records.

Failure conditions: The process can fail because of blocked company files, unavailable quota, incompatible occupation, active previous permit, poor document quality, missing qualification recognition, regulated-profession approval, medical unfitness or inconsistent personal data.
