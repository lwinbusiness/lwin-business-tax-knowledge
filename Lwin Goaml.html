EnglishWhat this portal does

It supports registration readiness, report-type triage, risk scoring, sector red-flag review, case-based decision practice and preparation of an English factual narrative draft.

Critical limitation: This tool does not replace the UAE FIU portal, your supervisory authority, legal advice, or an MLRO decision. It must not be used to auto-file, auto-clear a sanctions match, or justify not reporting.
Module 02

Applicability & Supervisory Scope

Not every UAE company is automatically a goAML reporting entity. The licensed activity and regulator determine scope.

MOET-supervised DNFBPs

Real estate brokers/agents, DPMS, independent accountants/auditors, and company/trust service providers under mainland and commercial free-zone licensing, subject to the applicable framework.

MOJ-supervised professions

Lawyers, notaries and relevant independent legal professionals, when carrying out the specified activities.

Other regulators

Financial institutions, securities firms, VASPs, financial-free-zone entities and gaming operators follow their licensing/supervisory authority in addition to federal requirements.

Common error: “General Trading” or an ordinary service company is not automatically a DNFBP merely because it is licensed in the UAE. Conversely, a company-formation/registered-office business may be a TCSP even when its licence wording looks like ordinary consultancy. Assess the actual activity, not only the label.
Module 03

goAML Registration Workflow

MOET-style two-stage workflow based on the 29 October 2024 registration guide. Other regulators may request different supporting documents.

Progress 0%

Stage 1 — SACM + Authenticator

Prepare one PDF

Trade licence + CO/MLRO authorisation letter + Emirates ID and/or passport. For the MOET guide: combine as one PDF, maximum 5 MB.

Submit SACM pre-registration

Select Reporting Entity, legal company name, supervisory body, licence number, and CO/MLRO details. UAE mobile format in the guide: 009715xxxxxxxx.

Verify email

The request does not reach the regulator until the email is verified. Whitelist no-reply.sacm@uaefiu.gov.ae and no-reply.goaml@uaefiu.gov.ae.

Regulator review

Correct rejection reasons before resubmitting. Do not create duplicate requests to bypass a rejection.

Generate secret key

Use the email OTP and SMS OTP. The 2024 guide states the OTP is valid for 24 hours. Each individual must have a separate secret key.

Configure Google Authenticator

Store the secret securely. The six-digit code is used for the SACM first-level login.

Stage 2 — Organisation Registration

Open goAML through UAE FIU services

First-level login: SACM username + authenticator code.

Register New Organisation

Select Reporting Entity and complete organisation, phone, address, CO/MLRO and passport/ID details.

Upload supporting file

Use complete, legible and consistent documents. Mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk.

Submit and retain reference

The system produces a request reference such as REP… Keep it in the compliance register.

Receive Organisation ID

After approval, retain the unique Org ID. Additional users register as persons under the active organisation.

Set roles and access

Grant only necessary permissions; promptly disable departed users on both SACM and goAML.

Module 04

Report Decision Wizard

A triage tool, not an automated legal conclusion. The MLRO must review the facts, current report permissions and regulator instructions.

1. Did the FIU send a request for additional information?
FIU Message Board/IEMS မှ အချက်အလက်တောင်းထားပါသလား။
Was the request initiated by the FIU?
2. Is the FIU request linked to your earlier report, or is it a broader enquiry?
3. Does the requested response include transactions?
2. Is this a sanctions / Targeted Financial Sanctions match?
3. Is the match confirmed or only potential/partial?
3. Is a sector activity-report trigger met?

Examples: REAR cash/VA property trigger or DPMSR threshold trigger.

4. Is there suspicion or reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF/PF?
5. Was the suspicious transaction executed?
No threshold rule: A suspicious transaction of AED 100 may require reporting; a non-suspicious AED 1 million transaction does not become an STR merely because it is large.
Parallel reports: REAR/DPMSR may be required by sector trigger and an STR/SAR may also be required for suspicion.
Do not tip off: Never tell the customer that an STR/SAR was filed or that an investigation is underway.

Module 08

CDD, EDD & Ongoing Monitoring

A high-level operational sequence aligned with the 2025 Executive Regulation. Sector/regulator rules can add more detail.

1. Identify & verify

  • Natural person identity, nationality, address, DOB/place of birth
  • Legal form, registered office, constitutional documents, tax/reference data
  • Authority of the person acting for the customer

2. Determine UBO & control

  • Natural person owning 25% or more
  • If unclear, identify control by other means
  • If still none, identify relevant senior management
  • Map trusts/legal arrangements and all relevant parties

3. Purpose & expected activity

  • Nature and intended purpose of relationship
  • Business model and ownership/control structure
  • Expected transaction size, frequency, counterparties and geography

4. Screen

  • UAE/UN targeted financial sanctions
  • PEP, relatives and close associates
  • Adverse media and internal watchlists
  • PF/dual-use and sector-specific risks

5. Apply EDD where high risk

  • More identity/occupation/UBO information
  • Source of funds and source of wealth
  • More frequent updates and monitoring
  • Senior-management approval
  • First payment from customer-name account where appropriate

6. Monitor & refresh

  • Compare actual activity with expected profile
  • Review high-risk customers more frequently
  • Investigate alerts and document closure/escalation
  • Retain reconstructable records for at least five years
CDD မပြီးနိုင်လျှင်: Business relationship ကို မစတင်/မဆက်၊ transaction ကို မလုပ်ရ။ Suspicion ရှိလျှင် STR စဉ်းစားပါ။ CDD ပြုလုပ်ခြင်းက customer ကို alert လုပ်နိုင်သည်ဟု အကြောင်းပြချက်ရှိလျှင် CDD မဆက်ဘဲ အကြောင်းပြချက်ပါ STR တင်နိုင်သည်။
If CDD cannot be completed: Do not establish or continue the relationship and do not execute the transaction. Consider an STR where necessary. If further CDD would alert the customer, the Regulation allows reporting the reasons for not applying it.
Module 09

Compliance Governance & MLRO Operating Model

The compliance function must be demonstrably effective, not merely named on paper.

Management level

CO/MLRO should have appropriate seniority, competence and experience.

Independence

Independent decision-making, direct access to senior management/board, and conflict controls.

Resources

Access to data, staff, monitoring, sanctions tools and sufficient budget.

No sales conflict

The 2026 joint guidance warns against day-to-day sales/customer relationship responsibility.

Minimum operating cycle

FrequencyRequired evidence
OngoingAlert review, sanctions screening, STR/SAR decisions, current KYC/UBO
MonthlyHigh-risk customer review, overdue CDD, alert backlog, report status
QuarterlyRisk metrics, training completion, control issues and remediation
Semi-annualCO/MLRO compliance report to senior management and relevant supervisor as applicable under 2026 joint guidance
Annual / triggerBusiness-wide risk assessment, policy refresh, independent audit/testing, training plan

Stop / Start / Increase

Stop: generic policies, shared logins, undocumented alert closures, nationality-only risk ratings, and threshold-only STR decisions.
Start: evidence-based customer risk assessment, UBO mapping, case logs, report quality review and current-list verification.
Increase: senior-management challenge, sector-specific training, PF controls, independent testing and monitoring of high-risk customers.

Module 12

goAML vs IEMS

These are separate systems. Mixing their purpose creates missed deadlines and incorrect submissions.

AreagoAMLIEMS
Primary purposeSubmit suspicious and specified sector/TFS reports; communicate through Message BoardExecute and respond to enquiries, prosecution decisions and other domestic-authority requests
Typical usersCO/MLRO and authorised reporting usersAdmin, Maker and Checker
WorkflowCreate, validate, submit report; respond to FIU requests with the appropriate report typeMaker prepares response/attachments; Checker authorises or rejects; Admin manages users
Critical disciplineCorrect report type, factual narrative, no tipping off, report without delayFollow due date, FIU comments, checklist, requested period and file format exactly
IEMS operational warning from the uploaded guide: pay strict attention to due dates and FIU comments; provide account statements for the requested period in CSV and PDF where requested; implement freeze orders immediately according to the instruction and case scope.
